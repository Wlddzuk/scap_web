# Clipper music loops

These three instrumental loops were generated procedurally for Clipper from
simple synthesized tones. They contain no samples, vocals, or third-party
recordings and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

- `cosmic-pulse.wav` — soft low pulse and open fifth
- `discovery-glow.wav` — bright ambient triad
- `quiet-momentum.wav` — restrained rhythmic science bed

Clipper loops a track to the narration length, ducks it from roughly -12 dBFS
to -22 dBFS during spoken words, then applies the final video fade-in/out.
